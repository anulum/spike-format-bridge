# Community conduct

Treat contributors and recording subjects with respect. Discuss reproducible
technical findings without harassment or personal attacks. Do not disclose
confidential data, credentials or identifying recording content.

Maintainer Miroslav Šotek handles conduct reports privately through
protoscience@anulum.li. Reports are reviewed in context; removal of abusive
content or access restrictions may be used to protect participants.

Technical disagreement is welcome when supported by source, explicit assumptions
and reproducible evidence.
