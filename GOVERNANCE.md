# Governance

Miroslav Šotek owns and maintains Spike Format Bridge. Changes require review of
their actual source, declared scope, tests and documentation. Claims about
supported recording formats require complete producer and consumer evidence.

Spike Format Bridge owns conversion and loss accounting. Spike Log owns its
native recording container; Evidence Manifest Toolkit owns shared sealing and
verification infrastructure. Data-quality judgement and device capture remain
separate responsibilities.

Local source acceptance does not authorise remote publication, releases,
recording capture or physical device operations. Licence changes and commercial
agreements require the rightsholder's decision.
